Governance & Accountability

A focus on transparency, accessibility, responsible representation and meaningful engagement with communities.

Good Governance & Effective Representation

Good governance requires openness, accountability, accessibility and attention to the needs of the people. This page presents the governance principles outlined for the Idah Federal Constituency.

✓

Accountability

Promote responsible representation and clear communication concerning constituency activities and interventions.

◉

Accessibility

Maintain an open channel through which community concerns, ideas and development priorities can be communicated.

▣

Transparency

Encourage openness around constituency projects, initiatives and development-related activities.

◎

Community Engagement

Encourage regular interaction with communities and stakeholders concerning local development needs.

⚖

Effective Representation

Present community concerns and development priorities through appropriate legislative and institutional channels.

∞

Inclusive Development

Promote an approach that recognizes the interests and needs of communities, families, young people and vulnerable groups.

Accountability

Accountability is an important part of responsible public representation. Information concerning constituency initiatives should be communicated as clearly as possible.

  • Encourage transparency in constituency interventions.
  • Provide accessible information about relevant activities.
  • Listen to community concerns and development priorities.
  • Maintain responsible communication with constituents.
  • Promote responsible use of public resources and opportunities.

Community Engagement

Effective representation requires communication between communities and their representatives.

Community engagement can provide an avenue for residents, traditional institutions, community organizations, professionals, women, young people and other stakeholders to communicate development needs.

Listening

Pay attention to issues raised by communities and stakeholders.

Dialogue

Encourage constructive discussions around development priorities.

Feedback

Create opportunities for communities to communicate concerns and observations.

Follow-Up

Follow relevant issues through appropriate government and institutional channels.

Effective Representation

Representation involves communicating the interests and concerns of constituents through the appropriate legislative and institutional processes.

Key Areas

  • Community development concerns.
  • Infrastructure needs.
  • Education and human capital development.
  • Healthcare and social development.
  • Agriculture and rural economic development.
  • Youth and women empowerment.
  • Peace, unity and community development.

Transparency

Transparency helps communities understand how development priorities are identified and communicated.

The website provides dedicated sections for development priorities, news, media, resources and other public information.

Governance Principles

Accountability • Transparency • Accessibility • Community Engagement • Responsible Representation • Inclusive Development
