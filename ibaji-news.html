Hon. Uchola Empowers Over 200 Women in Onyedega, Ibaji LGA

By Ibaji Television

More than 200 women in Onyedega, Ibaji Local Government Area, have benefited from an empowerment initiative by Hon. Uchola, in a programme focused on economic opportunity, livelihood support and grassroots community engagement.

IBAJI TELEVISION — SPECIAL REPORT: A major grassroots empowerment programme has taken place in Onyedega, Ibaji Local Government Area, bringing together more than 200 women and community stakeholders in an initiative focused on economic opportunity and improved livelihoods.

The programme provided an opportunity for women in the community to receive support aimed at strengthening their various economic activities and improving their ability to contribute to the welfare of their families.

Supporting Women at the Grassroots

Women play an important role in the economic and social life of communities across Ibaji. Many are involved in agriculture, trading, small-scale businesses and other livelihood activities. Initiatives directed towards women provide an avenue for beneficiaries to strengthen their existing activities and pursue additional economic opportunities.

Speaking during the programme, Hon. Uchola emphasized the importance of supporting women at the grassroots and creating opportunities that can improve livelihoods and strengthen families.

Beneficiaries Appreciate the Initiative

The women who participated in the programme expressed appreciation for the support, noting that empowerment initiatives of this nature can assist beneficiaries in improving their economic activities and responding to the needs of their families.

Community members and stakeholders also welcomed the programme, highlighting the importance of sustained engagement with residents and initiatives that directly address local economic and community-development needs.

A Focus on Community Engagement

Beyond the immediate empowerment activity, the gathering also provided an opportunity for interaction among women, community stakeholders and other participants.

Such grassroots engagements provide opportunities for residents to discuss issues affecting their communities and to participate in conversations concerning economic development and local opportunities.

The empowerment of more than 200 women in Onyedega represents a community-focused initiative, with attention placed on women's participation, livelihood opportunities and grassroots development.

Ibaji Television Reports

Ibaji Television was on the ground in Onyedega to capture the event and the participation of women from the community.

The programme highlights the importance of initiatives that connect development efforts with residents at the grassroots and create opportunities for women to participate meaningfully in economic and community activities.

Residents and community stakeholders are encouraged to remain informed, participate constructively in community affairs and continue engaging with leaders and organizations on issues affecting their communities.
