Meet Victor Ojogbane

Omale Victor Ojogbane is an APC candidate for the House of Representatives representing the Idah Federal Constituency.

He is from Unale, Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State, and his background is rooted in the community and people of the constituency.

This website provides public information about his background, political activities, stated priorities, public engagements and updates.
