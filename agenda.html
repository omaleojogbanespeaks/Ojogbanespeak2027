7-Point Agenda

A New Vision for Development, Opportunity and Responsible Representation

A development-focused agenda for Idah Federal Constituency built around education, empowerment, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture, good governance and community development.

Our 7-Point Agenda

The people of Idah Federal Constituency deserve representation that listens, acts and delivers. Our agenda is built around the real needs of our communities and a commitment to creating opportunities for every citizen.

1 Education & Human Capital Development
  • Support students with scholarships, bursaries and educational materials.
  • Advocate for the rehabilitation and equipping of schools across the constituency.
  • Establish skills-acquisition and vocational training programmes for young people.
  • Promote digital literacy, ICT training and access to modern learning opportunities.
  • Support outstanding students and young innovators with mentorship and opportunities.
2 Youth Empowerment & Employment
  • Create practical empowerment programmes for unemployed and underemployed youths.
  • Support entrepreneurship, start-ups and small businesses with training and access to funding opportunities.
  • Establish vocational programmes in areas such as technology, tailoring, welding, agriculture, catering and other profitable trades.
  • Build partnerships with government agencies, private organisations and development partners to create jobs and internships.
3 Agriculture, Fisheries & Rural Economic Development
  • Promote modern farming, fisheries and livestock production.
  • Support farmers and cooperatives with inputs, equipment, training and market access.
  • Advocate for improved rural roads to connect farmers to markets.
  • Encourage agro-processing and value addition so our people can earn more from their agricultural products.
  • Promote agriculture as a sustainable source of employment and wealth creation.
4 Infrastructure & Basic Amenities
  • Advocate for the rehabilitation and construction of roads connecting communities.
  • Prioritise access to clean and reliable water through boreholes and other sustainable water projects.
  • Support rural electrification and renewable-energy initiatives.
  • Advocate for better drainage and community environmental infrastructure.
  • Push for projects that directly improve the daily lives of residents.
5 Healthcare & Social Development
  • Advocate for better-equipped primary healthcare centres across the constituency.
  • Support periodic medical outreach programmes for vulnerable residents.
  • Promote maternal and child healthcare.
  • Support access to essential medicines and basic medical services.
  • Develop programmes for persons with disabilities, widows, elderly citizens and other vulnerable groups.
6 Good Governance, Accountability & Effective Representation
  • Maintain an open and accessible relationship with the people.
  • Establish regular constituency engagement and town-hall meetings.
  • Promote transparency in constituency projects and interventions.
  • Ensure the needs of communities are properly presented and followed up at the National Assembly.
  • Champion legislation and policies that protect the interests and development of Idah Federal Constituency.
7 Community Development, Security & Unity
  • Promote peace, unity and cooperation among communities across the constituency.
  • Support community-based initiatives that strengthen security and peaceful coexistence.
  • Encourage youth participation in community development.
  • Work with traditional institutions, religious leaders, community organisations and other stakeholders.
  • Build a constituency where every community has a voice and every citizen has a stake in development.

Our Commitment

Victor Omale Ojogbane believes representation should not be about promises alone—it should be about service, accountability, accessibility and measurable results.

Together, we can build an Idah Federal Constituency where education creates opportunity, agriculture creates prosperity, infrastructure connects communities, healthcare protects families, and good governance serves the people.
