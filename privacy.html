Privacy Policy

Your privacy and responsible handling of personal information are important to us.

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the official website of Omale Victor Ojogbane, APC House of Representatives Candidate for Idah Federal Constituency.

This Privacy Policy explains how information provided through this website may be collected, used and protected.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use this website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number.
  • The contents of messages submitted through contact forms.
  • Other information you voluntarily choose to provide.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for communication and website-related purposes.

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and messages.
  • Communicate with individuals who contact the website.
  • Respond to requests for information.
  • Improve the website and visitor experience.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the website.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell or rent personal information submitted through this website.

Information may only be shared where reasonably necessary to respond to a request, operate the website, comply with applicable legal requirements, or protect the security and rights of the website and its users.

5. Cookies and Website Technologies

This website may use basic technologies that help pages function correctly or improve website performance.

If third-party services such as video platforms, social media, analytics services or externally hosted images are used, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to external websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content or policies of websites operated by third parties.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information to them.

7. Data Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

9. Your Privacy Choices

If you have voluntarily submitted personal information through this website and would like to ask about the information provided, you may contact us using the details below.

Where applicable, you may request correction or deletion of information that you have provided.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of online services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the website, its services or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information submitted through this website is handled, please contact us.
