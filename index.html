About Victor Omale Ojogbane

Background information and constituency focus.

House of Representatives Candidate

Victor Omale Ojogbane is an APC candidate for the House of Representatives representing the Idah Federal Constituency.

He is from Unale, Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State.

His stated development priorities cover education, youth empowerment, agriculture, infrastructure, healthcare, good governance and community development.

Constituency:
Idah Federal Constituency, Kogi State, Nigeria.

Service, Accountability & Development

Representation should focus on accessibility, accountability, responsible leadership and measurable community development.

Development Priorities

Seven key areas outlined in the development agenda.

1

Education & Human Capital

Scholarships, educational materials, school improvement, skills acquisition, digital literacy and opportunities for young people.

2

Youth Empowerment

Entrepreneurship, vocational training, start-up support, internships and employment opportunities.

3

Agriculture & Fisheries

Support for farmers, fishermen, cooperatives, modern production, agro-processing and market access.

4

Infrastructure

Roads, clean water, electricity, drainage and other basic amenities that support communities.

5

Healthcare

Better-equipped primary healthcare, medical outreach, maternal and child healthcare and essential services.

6

Good Governance

Accessibility, transparency, constituency engagement, accountability and effective representation.

7

Community & Unity

Peace, unity, community participation and cooperation with traditional, religious and community stakeholders.

7-Point Agenda
