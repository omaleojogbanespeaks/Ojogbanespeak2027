Mission & Vision

Building stronger communities through responsible representation, development and opportunity.

A Vision for Community Development

Our focus is on issues that directly affect the daily lives, opportunities and wellbeing of people across the Idah Federal Constituency.

Our Mission

To promote responsible representation, community development, inclusive opportunities and constructive engagement on issues affecting the people of the Idah Federal Constituency.

Our Vision

To contribute to a constituency where communities have access to essential infrastructure, quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities and meaningful participation in development.

Our Development Priorities

Key areas of community infrastructure and empowerment.

Road Infrastructure

Supporting attention to roads and transportation infrastructure that connect communities and facilitate movement, commerce and access to essential services.

Water & Boreholes

Supporting access to clean and reliable water through community-focused water infrastructure and borehole initiatives.

Education

Promoting improved educational opportunities and supporting initiatives that help children and young people gain knowledge and useful skills.

Healthcare

Giving attention to healthcare needs and initiatives that can improve access to essential health services within communities.

Markets & Local Economy

Supporting local commerce, markets and economic activity that provide opportunities for traders, farmers, artisans and small businesses.

Women Empowerment

Promoting opportunities for women through skills development, entrepreneurship, economic participation and community engagement.

Youth Empowerment

Supporting young people through skills development, entrepreneurship, education and opportunities for constructive participation in community development.

Key Areas of Focus

  • Improved road and transportation infrastructure
  • Access to clean and reliable water
  • Education and skills development
  • Accessible healthcare services
  • Market and local economic development
  • Women empowerment and economic participation
  • Youth empowerment and skills development
  • Inclusive community engagement
  • Responsible and accountable representation
