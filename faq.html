Frequently Asked Questions

Below are some common questions about the candidate, his proposed agenda and ways to access more information.

Who is Victor Omale Ojogbane?

Victor Omale Ojogbane is an APC candidate for the House of Representatives for the Idah/Igalamela/Ofu/Ibaji Federal Constituency.

Which constituency does he seek to represent?

He seeks to represent the Idah/Igalamela/Ofu/Ibaji Federal Constituency in the House of Representatives.

What are the major areas of his proposed agenda?

The seven-point agenda focuses on education and human capital development, youth empowerment and employment, agriculture and rural economic development, infrastructure and basic amenities, healthcare and social development, good governance and effective representation, as well as community development, security and unity.

What does the education agenda focus on?

The proposed education priorities include scholarships and bursaries, educational materials, rehabilitation and equipping of schools, skills acquisition, vocational training, digital literacy and ICT opportunities.

What is proposed for young people?

The agenda proposes practical empowerment programmes, entrepreneurship support, vocational training and partnerships that can create opportunities for employment, internships and small-business development.

What is the agricultural development plan?

The proposed approach includes support for farmers and cooperatives, agricultural inputs and equipment, training, improved market access, rural roads, fisheries, livestock and agro-processing.

What infrastructure priorities are proposed?

The agenda identifies roads, clean and reliable water, boreholes, rural electrification, renewable energy, drainage and other community infrastructure as key areas of attention.

What healthcare improvements are proposed?

The healthcare agenda includes better-equipped primary healthcare centres, periodic medical outreach, maternal and child healthcare, essential medicines and support for vulnerable groups.

How will citizens engage with the representative?

The proposed governance approach includes constituency engagement, town-hall meetings, accessibility and regular interaction with communities to better understand and follow up on community needs.

What does the agenda say about accountability?

The agenda places emphasis on transparency in constituency projects and interventions, accessibility and properly presenting and following up community needs at the National Assembly.
