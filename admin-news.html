📌 How to Publish Your News

  1. Fill in the news headline.
  2. Select the date.
  3. Paste the URL of your news picture.
  4. Select the news category.
  5. Write your short description.
  6. Click Generate News Code.
  7. Click Copy News Code.
  8. Open news.html in GitHub.
  9. Paste the generated news card inside the news-grid section.
  10. Save/commit the changes.
  11. Your new news story will appear on the website.
